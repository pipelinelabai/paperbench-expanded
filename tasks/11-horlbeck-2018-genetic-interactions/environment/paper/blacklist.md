Do not obtain or reuse the authors' analysis implementations, analysis notebooks, task reference solutions, processed phenotype/GI tables, predicted interaction maps, or previously generated task outputs. The complete paper and its references remain available for method recovery. These restrictions do not prohibit using or citing the scientific method described in the paper.
