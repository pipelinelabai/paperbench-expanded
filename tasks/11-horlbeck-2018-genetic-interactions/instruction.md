Your study must include:

- The replicate-averaged gene-level map, continuous profiles, and numerical figure evidence within the supplied data scope.
- Independently reconstructed biological replicates and the gene-level comparisons represented by Figures 2D and 2E.
- A systematic guide-omission study that measures sensitivity where a perturbation acts, not only over the whole map.
- A replicated-identity matching null and conclusions supported by the observed results, including negative or inconclusive findings.

The guide-omission and matching-null experiments are benchmark-defined validation studies, not claims about the paper's original Figure 2 procedure. This is not a full-paper reproduction, and no unavailable functional-module labels are required.
