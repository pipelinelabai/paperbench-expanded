# Geometry or momentum? A bounded syn-CPP steering experiment

Using Cao, Kong and Tantillo's paper on post-transition-state bifurcations, determine how fixed syn-CPP TSre and TSsi starting states move toward the three specified product directions over 40 fs. Then test, on two preselected 20 fs cases, whether reversing the transverse thermal momentum changes that motion while holding geometry and the forward reactive-mode kick fixed.

The deliverable is a reproducible, numerically qualified comparison, not reproduction of the paper's terminal product percentages. Use the supplied immutable initial states, actual GPU4PySCF energies/gradients and the finite experiment in `/home/paper/addendum.md`. Choose an efficient implementation and defensible diagnostics; do not choose favorable seeds, classifications or trajectories.
